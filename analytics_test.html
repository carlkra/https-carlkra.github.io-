What is GA4 event tracking? ▼
GA4 event tracking captures user interactions on your website to help you understand how visitors engage with your content.
How does parameter tracking work? ▼
Parameters provide additional context about events, such as page location, element type, and user actions.
What are custom events? ▼
Custom events are tracking points you define based on specific user interactions unique to your website.
